## 9. Methodological implications

Two important QA lessons from this checkpoint:

1. **Placement novelty != person novelty.**
   A person newly appearing in a newly added cohort is not a new canonical person if already present in another cohort.

2. **Field corrections must propagate deterministically upward.**
   A corrected individual/field snapshot must regenerate person aggregate and cross-cohort metrics; manually copied aggregate values are vulnerable to staleness.

Accordingly, longitudinal common metrics v0.2 are now row-derived.
