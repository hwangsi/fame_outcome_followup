# Dong-A 2010 → 2020 unresolved taxonomy v1.0

Generated: 2026-08-18

Generic `pending` should not remain indefinitely. After structured re-search, unresolved rows are classified by why they remain unresolved so missing evidence is not silently converted into failure.

## Resolution states

### `active_role_unresolved`
Identity is secure, but dated 2019–2021 evidence does not establish the active role strongly enough. A pre-window role or current profile cannot be automatically back-cast to 2020. `scope_score=null`.

### `identity_ambiguous`
Target-window searches produce plausible same-name candidates but career continuity from the 2010 identity anchor cannot be proven. Do not merge by name alone. Preserve candidates separately. `scope_score=null`.

### `no_active_public_role_evidence_in_window`
Identity is secure and a structured search was completed, but evidence found concerns former status, litigation, financial/property status, reprints, or other facts that do not establish an active professional/public role. This does NOT mean unemployed, unsuccessful, or score 0. `scope_score=null`.

### `role_ambiguous_after_exit`
A documented resignation, retirement, removal, or departure occurred before the target window, but evidence for a subsequent role is conflicting or insufficient. `scope_score=null` unless later resolved.

## Analysis treatment

Unresolved rows remain explicit missing observations, not failures. After freeze, report resolved-denominator results, unresolved proportions overall/by category, competing events separately, and sensitivity bounds rather than assigning unresolved rows score 0.

## Search-completion rule

A row can be closed unresolved after checking the T0 identity anchor, exact-year searches, nearest-window searches, likely institutional/archive sources, and same-name contamination. New evidence may reopen a row in a later version.
